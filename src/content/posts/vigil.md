Vigil is a personal training app with an AI coach and physiotherapist that knows your body: it reads your injury history, goals, equipment and every logged session, then writes workouts grounded in eight training and rehab books and tells you each day whether to rest or train.

You talk to it in plain language to plan, change and log sessions, and it remembers what you tell it, alongside a private journal and a meditation timer for the rest of the day.

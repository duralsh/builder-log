The fourth session in the **series**. **Connectors (MCP)** are how Claude reaches your real tools — Notion, Microsoft 365, Slack — used on their own and then chained together. For everyone on Claude.ai or Cowork. No coding.

### Three workflows, walked through as flow charts

- **Answer Finder** — searches Notion + Slack + Outlook at once and returns one cited answer, plus *who to ask* for more.
- **Meeting Prep** — calendar + Notion + Slack → a one-page brief before any meeting.
- **Salesforce churn alert** — pulls churn signals from Salesforce, then DMs each account owner on Slack and writes a timestamped report to Notion.

### The principle: read freely, act on purpose

The thread running through all of it: anything that *reads* can fire automatically, but any workflow that **writes** should be a manual command — never something that auto-fires. The session also handed out three real, copy-and-use skills (`answer-finder`, `meeting-prep`, and the manual `salesforce-churn-alert` command) demonstrating exactly that boundary, each one preflighting its connectors before it does anything.

Vigil is a personal coaching app. It writes each session from what you are training for, what your body has been through, and the equipment you actually have.

Injury history drives the programming. A case file per region — old tears, surgeries, what a side will carry and what it will not — is read before every session rather than compiled once into a fixed rule, so the compensations end when the weak side catches up instead of outliving it.

The chat has a memory. What you tell the coach, and how you rate each session you log, both feed back in, so the next block reflects how the last one actually went rather than how it was planned.

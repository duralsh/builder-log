Prägen is a German language learning platform built for serious learners — now a full product with a web app and native **iOS** and **Android** apps, all sharing one backend.

Look up any German or English word and get an AI-generated grammar breakdown with conjugation tables, case requirements, CEFR levels, and example sentences. Save words to your personal **Wortschatz**, drill them with **spaced-repetition flashcards** (SM-2 scheduling), and read **graded German articles** scraped daily from real news sources — Nachrichten Leicht (A1–A2), Tagesschau (B1–B2), and Deutsche Welle (B2–C1) — with sentence-level translations.

### Highlights

- **AI word lookup** — deep grammar breakdowns backed by a 466K-lemma offline dictionary with fuzzy "did you mean" suggestions.
- **Flashcards & quizzes** — practice from your own vocabulary, a 20K-word bank, or Goethe A1–B1 lists; plus multiple-choice and *Der/Die/Das* gender quizzes.
- **Graded reading** — daily articles with token annotations and native text-to-speech pronunciation.
- **Cross-platform** — Next.js web frontend, Flutter mobile apps, and a FastAPI backend serving the same REST API to all clients.
